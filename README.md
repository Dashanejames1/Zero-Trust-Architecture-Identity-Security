## 🔑 Technical Notes

CVSS severity bands: None (0.0) · Low (0.1–3.9) · Medium (4.0–6.9) · High (7.0–8.9) · Critical (9.0–10.0).
Scope change is easy to miss and heavily affects the score — it applies when exploiting one component lets the attacker affect resources beyond that component's security authority.
Temporal and Environmental metrics only ever lower or hold the Base score in practical terms for a defender weighting their own environment — they refine it, they don't invent new severity out of nothing.
